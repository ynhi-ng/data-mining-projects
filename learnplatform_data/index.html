Problem & context

District engagement files + product / district metadata

The notebook merges LearnPlatform engagement CSVs, cleans engagement index and percent access, and joins product and district attributes to ask how digital learning behaved before and after the COVID shock — and for whom.

Timing & weekly rhythm

  • Engagement is high Mon–Thu, softens Friday, and drops on weekends.
  • Outbreak weeks (early 2020) show a sharp rise in digital engagement as schooling moved online.
  • Pre-outbreak engagement was already non-trivial — consistent with a digital-device baseline.

Products & providers

  • Major providers in the extract include Google, Microsoft, and IXL Learning.
  • Google Docs / Classroom stand out on average engagement and learning-management use.
  • Learning platforms and courseware peak near late March; some content-creation tools rise later.

Districts & equity signals

  • Internet access and engagement move together — stable access supports higher engagement.
  • Negative association between engagement and share of Black/Hispanic students in a district.
  • Negative association between engagement and free/reduced lunch share.
  • Black/Hispanic share and free/reduced lunch are positively related — poverty and racial composition co-occur in the data.

Limits & next steps

  • Observational aggregates; correlations are not causal.
  • Large raw engagement files stay local; this report carries the narrative findings.
  • Next: add chart exports from notebook aggregates for interactive views.
