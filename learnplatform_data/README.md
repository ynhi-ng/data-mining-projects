# LearnPlatform — Digital Learning Engagement EDA

## Summary
Exploratory analysis of K–12 digital learning engagement during 2020: how product
usage and access changed around the COVID outbreak, which platforms dominated, and
how engagement relates to district demographics.

## Data & methodology
- Merge district engagement files; join product and district info
- Weekday vs weekend; pre- vs post-outbreak; product leaders; equity-gap views

## Data (not in git)
Keep locally:
- `districts_info.csv`, `products_info.csv`
- `engagement_data/` — per-district engagement CSVs

**Source:** [LearnPlatform COVID-19 Impact on Digital Learning](https://www.kaggle.com/c/learnplatform-covid19-impact-on-digital-learning)
(Kaggle / LearnPlatform challenge data). Download from Kaggle; do not commit the files.

## Limits & next steps
- Observational aggregates; correlations ≠ causation
- Report is the shareable narrative; raw engagement stays local
