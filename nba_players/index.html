NBA Players

Career and season-level patterns across historical NBA player and performance tables — a readable summary of the notebook analysis.

Problem & context

What shows up when biographical career attributes are joined with season-by-season performance?

The analysis prepares two public-style tables — players (career span, position, college, physical attributes) and seasons (year, team, minutes, efficiency, shooting splits) — then explores overview distributions and deeper performance views.

Position mix (career table)

Guards and forwards dominate roster history in this extract

Key findings

  • About 4,550 player records spanning careers from the late 1940s through 2018, with 473 distinct colleges represented.
  • Season table holds ~24.7k rows (1950–2017), suitable for year-over-year and team-level efficiency comparisons.
  • Primary positions concentrate in G and F, with hybrid labels (F-C, G-F, etc.) capturing role flexibility.
  • Extreme single-season efficiency outliers exist in small-sample rows — any PER leaderboard needs minute filters before storytelling.
